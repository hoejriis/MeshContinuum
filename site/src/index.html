What MECON adds

One Reader, more reception

See traffic captured by the sources available to your deployment. Keep one message with its reception evidence, even when several observers hear it.

Manage devices remotely

Check connectivity and health, configure supported devices and send through an authorised Companion gateway. The actions available depend on the device.

Your infrastructure, your control

MECON is free and intended for open-source release. Run it hosted, at home, or across several sites. mecon.cloud is one deployment, not a required service.

New to MeshCore?

MeshCore is open-source firmware for small LoRa radios that pass messages to each other over long distances, with no mobile network or server. MECON is built around it.

Companion
Your personal radio, paired with an app on your phone or computer.
Repeater
A radio left on in a good spot, forwarding others' messages to extend the mesh.
Room server
A small bulletin board on the mesh that keeps posts for you.
Observer
A radio that also reports what it hears to an online service. MECON reads from observers as well as your own radios.

MeshCore in brief explains the rest, and what you need to begin.

How it fits together

MECON is an application layer around MeshCore. It does not replace MeshCore's packet formats, radio routing, node identities or normal Companion apps. Everything inside the dashed boundary is optional; the MeshCore radios keep talking to each other without it.

Ways to run it

From someone else's server to infrastructure of your own. In every case your radios keep working as ordinary MeshCore radios if MECON goes away. Deployment choices

Hosted

A hosted service runs the Backend, Reader and broker. You bring your Companion, and optionally a gateway radio at home.

A home site

One small computer on your network runs the Backend, Reader and a local broker. Nothing has to be reachable from the Internet.

Several sites

Each site runs its own Backend, and they share records as peers, with brokers on the Internet for reach and resilience.

The firmware alone

Run mecon-firmware with a manager of your own, using its open device contracts.

Getting involved

Read the documentation, review the device contracts in mecon-firmware, describe a use case, or express interest in testing. Documentation feedback and contract review are welcome now.
