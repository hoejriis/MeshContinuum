A stronger mesh network, without giving up control.

MeshContinuum (MECON) helps people and communities connect, understand and manage decentralized radio messaging networks.

Use the radios you already have, keep communicating when the Internet is unavailable, and add shared observation, authorized history and device management when you choose.

First, what is MeshCore?

MeshCore is a radio-based messaging system. Small, low-cost radios pass text messages from one to the next, so a message can cross a town or a valley without cellular service or the Internet. Two words you will meet:

Companion
The radio connected to your phone or computer. You read and write messages through it.
Repeater
A radio, often in a high spot, that relays messages so they travel beyond the reach of a single radio.

The radios talk to each other directly, with no server in between. A message can still be missed if a radio is out of range, asleep or disconnected. MeshCore in brief explains the rest, and what you need to begin.

What MECON adds

You do not need MECON to send MeshCore messages. It is an optional layer for people and communities who want more from the network they already have.

Keep conversations familiar

A Reader for your contacts, direct messages and channels, connected straight to your Companion, with optional authorized history online. It tells you when a message is queued, transmitted or acknowledged by the radio network, and never invents a read receipt.

Make more of what the network hears

One radio hears only what reaches it while it is on. MECON combines authorized reception evidence from several locations, so a message heard elsewhere can still reach your history. It cannot promise perfect coverage or delivery.

See how the network performs

Understand device health, reception and radio conditions, with observation that respects privacy, and an optional public map of activity that shows no message content.

Manage radios responsibly

Fleet owners configure and maintain their devices, including ones on a hilltop. Owning a radio never grants access to other people's private conversations.

Run it your way

Self-host on a small machine, extend across several sites, or choose a hosted service. Your MeshCore radios keep working on their own either way. Ways to run it

How it fits together

The radio network comes first and works on its own. A MECON Backend is an optional service that gathers authorized observations, stores the history you are permitted to keep, and coordinates the management of your radios. The Reader can also talk to your Companion directly over Bluetooth.

The technical detail

Radios with Wi-Fi report what they hear through a message broker (MQTT). The broker can belong to your own site, be shared with others, or be a public one. A radio can also be connected by USB cable to the computer running the Reader. MECON does not change MeshCore's packet formats, radio routing or node identities, and ordinary MeshCore apps keep working. Architecture

MECON is an independent, non-profit project with no revenue objective. It is built to share what works, openly, so that others can use it, run it and build on it. It is not affiliated with the MeshCore project.
